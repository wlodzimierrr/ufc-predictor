## Why a new contract is necessary

The accepted validator in `modeling/phase5c1_evidence.py` requires explicit
title booleans and complete, identity-bound admitted-domain experience claims.
An HTML extraction or operator risk acceptance cannot honestly be converted
into a v1 provider assertion by generating `authoritative=true`, `complete=true`
or `verified_debut`.

There is a substantive model-semantic issue as well. In
`features/pipeline.py`, `both_debuting` derives from the two snapshot `is_debut`
values. In `features/debut_prior.py`, that flag controls three saved-prior
features. History scope also affects career counts, rates, rolling features,
opponent indexes and global Elo. Renaming a coverage field cannot establish
compatibility, even for fighters with some observed history. Missing histories
cannot be silently treated as zero experience, nor may saved preprocessing be
refit to accommodate a new evidence policy.

The proposal therefore separates source-observation facts, operator-adopted
interpretation/risk policy and computational bindings. A non-predictive semantic
review, explicit policy decisions and a new preregistration remain gates before
a future adapter may be implemented and frozen. Any incompatible feature
meaning requires a separately authorized modeling experiment, not a patched
saved bundle.

The recommended named approval bundle is **QLS1**: adopt decisions D1-D6 and D8
as written, require the D7 compatibility audit rather than assert it passed,
and authorize only that next documentation-only, no-model audit. It explicitly
accepts a lower-assurance source view and operator-defined ordinary-designation
title semantics, not certified source completeness. Zero-history targets remain
blocked initially. The audit may establish that an existing software feature is
an observed-zero-history proxy without proving actual career debut; admitting
those targets would still need the proposal's additional pre-outcome decision.
